## A first essay

This is a sample Essay Post rendered through the new Ink & Garden content
pipeline. It is markdown with MDX compilation — headings, paragraphs, and
inline images all compile at build time into a typed `Post` in the
`content-index`.

![A placeholder hero image alt-texted for accessibility](/content/p/hello-ink-garden/hero.png)

The pipeline parses this frontmatter with `gray-matter`, validates it against
the schema, and emits the body as a lazily-imported MDX component. No runtime
fetch — everything materializes at build time (AD-3).
