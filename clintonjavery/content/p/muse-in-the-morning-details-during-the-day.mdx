What do I mean by that? How can you make sure that you're getting everything done and that you don't miss anything important or waste time on things that don't matter?

In the morning you muse. Think about the high level stuff. Consider your goals and projects that you're working on. That's kind of like the "theory". Focus on a few big questions. Like, what are my goals, what projects am I working on. See things from this big picture perspective without looking too much at each detail.

Then throughout the day allow yourself to focus completely on whatever activity is in front of you. Do the details. No you'll have the freedom to focus completely on the details, because in the morning you took the big picture view and during that time you kind of calibrated your mind, so that you know that everything you are going to do for the day fits in with what you are trying to accomplish.

Give it a try with me and let me know how it works for you.
